The Four Failures

Your fleet GPS shows
what happened.
We show what to do.

Most fleet software is retrospective. Syndara is prescriptive. Every module delivers a ranked action queue, not another dashboard of charts.

01
Telematics: Data rich, insight poor
Every AIS-140 fleet has GPS and CAN bus data. But operators receive alerts after failures, not before. Breakdowns are reactive, maintenance is scheduled by calendar not by vehicle health, and four systems hold data that never talks to each other.
₹12–18% vehicle uptime lost to unplanned breakdowns
02
Driver scoring: Punitive, not prescriptive
Existing products output a score from 1 to 100. Drivers get fined or fired. No coaching system, no contextual adjustment, no behaviour change. Accident rates stay high, tyre wear costs stay invisible, and union relations deteriorate.
₹₹15,000–40,000 cost to replace each trained driver lost
03
EV charging: Unoptimised, expensive
Fleet operators with EVs manage charging the way they managed diesel — ad hoc. Vehicles charge at peak tariff hours. Batteries deep-discharge regularly. Charge levels don't match tomorrow's route schedule. Energy costs run 25–35% above minimum.
₹₹8–12L/year wasted on bad charge timing per 100-vehicle depot
04
Warranty claims: Denied, delayed, lost
OEM dealers deny 35–50% of legitimate warranty claims citing "driver misuse" or "out-of-spec operation." Operators have no continuous telemetry evidence to counter the claim. Documentation is scattered across WhatsApp and handwritten records.
₹₹40–80L/year in valid claims lost per 500-vehicle fleet
